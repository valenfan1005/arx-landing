Fed Holds. BTC Longs Cut Carry. HYPE Bulls Doubled Down.

29 July 2026 · 20:17 UTC HYPE BTC Funding Macro
Quick Take
In the four hours after Warsh held rates for the fifth time, BTC perp longs on Hyperliquid cut their carry exposure: funding fell from +10.4% to +6.4% APR, reducing the daily long-side cost from $651K to $391K. HYPE did the opposite — funding more than doubled from +5.02% to +10.95% APR as price slipped 1.5%, meaning longs accumulated positions at a higher carry cost as the price dipped. ETH shorts covered and funding flipped back to slightly positive. One FOMC hold, three different positioning choices.

BTC Longs De-Risked

BTC perp open interest on Hyperliquid stands at $2.23B at 20:17 UTC. With funding at +6.41% APR, longs are paying approximately $391,000 per day to maintain positions. Four hours earlier, that cost was $651K/day at +10.4% APR. The compression means longs reduced their net exposure as the FOMC decision played out — either by closing positions or via shorts entering and balancing the book.

Per CNBC, Fed Chairman Warsh said the Fed "won't hesitate to stop inflation" but that the bond market has doubts. Per Investing.com, the Fed held rates steady for the fifth consecutive meeting, with Warsh noting that the absence of forward guidance may have driven the rise in Treasury yields. A cautious macro backdrop; BTC longs responded by reducing carry exposure.

HYPE Bulls Accumulated Into the Dip

HYPE perp open interest stands at $1.22B, with funding at +10.95% APR — more than double the +5.02% APR recorded four hours ago. HYPE price fell from $54.53 to $53.71 (−1.5%) in the same window. The funding increase while price declined records that longs were net buyers during the dip: the book became more long-heavy even as the market marked lower.

Daily carry on HYPE longs rose from $168K to $366K in four hours. Longs on a $1.22B HYPE book now pay approximately $133,000 per 24-hour period more than they did at the prior snapshot. That cost is paid every day, regardless of price direction, for as long as the funding imbalance persists.

The Arithmetic

HYPE OI: $1.22B × 10.95% APR ÷ 365 = $366K/day from longs to shorts. BTC OI: $2.23B × 6.41% APR ÷ 365 = $391K/day. Despite BTC having 1.83× more open interest, longs on both pay nearly the same daily carry in dollar terms. HYPE bulls are paying 1.71× the carry cost per dollar of notional compared to BTC longs.

ETH: Shorts Covered, Funding Flipped

ETH funding stood at −2.29% APR at 16:17 UTC, meaning shorts were paying longs ~$117K/day to stay short. By 20:17 UTC, that flipped to +1.28% APR, with longs now paying shorts ~$64K/day. ETH OI is $1.83B; the magnitude of the current funding is small — the book is near-balanced. The direction shift records short-side pressure unwinding post-FOMC.

Earlier in the same session (04:17 UTC), ETH shorts were paying even more aggressively per the 04:17 UTC snapshot. The intraday ETH funding pattern today: heavy short pressure early → short covering into FOMC → slight positive lean post-decision. Price is essentially flat (−1.62% on the day at $1,889.60).

What to Watch

  • HYPE funding sustainability: At +0.01%/8h and $1.22B OI, longs pay $366K/day. If price doesn't recover, the carry cost erodes long-side returns. Watch whether funding stays above +0.008%/8h over the next 24h as a signal that conviction holds despite the cost.
  • BTC funding floor: The drop from 10.4% to 6.4% APR represents longs trimming exposure. If BTC funding continues compressing toward 2–3% APR, it records further de-risking. If it stabilizes or bounces from current levels, longs are likely done reducing.
  • Treasury yield reaction: Warsh's comment that no forward guidance drove the rise in Treasury yields adds duration risk to the macro picture. A sustained move higher in yields historically pressures both equities and risk assets. BTC's positioning response today (−3.99pp funding) may already reflect that expectation being priced in on Hyperliquid.
