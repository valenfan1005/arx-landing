Three TradFi Blockchain Moves in One Session

Per CoinTelegraph, Visa used its Q3 earnings call to outline a comprehensive stablecoin strategy, highlighting investments across OpenUSD, tokenized deposits, and AI-powered commerce infrastructure. The company is publicly committing to building across the full stablecoin stack on the same day traditional equity markets sold off on Fed uncertainty.

Per CoinDesk, BNY Mellon — the world's largest custodian — announced plans to target the $8.6T transfer agency market by adding a digital ownership record for tokenized funds on blockchain rails while maintaining its existing system. BNY is not replacing the traditional infrastructure; it is layering blockchain settlement on top.

Morgan Stanley

Per CoinDesk, Morgan Stanley executives stated the traditional 9-to-5 banking day is "officially dying" as tokenization and 24/7 markets reshape finance, making always-on banking a reality. The framing positions tokenized assets as the structural driver of the shift, not just a fintech add-on.

Tether also signed a tokenization deal with the Nairobi Securities Exchange, per CoinTelegraph, covering tokenized securities, blockchain-based market infrastructure, and potential use of USDT as a settlement layer. A developed-market equities exchange would draw more attention; this deal signals the tokenization wave reaching frontier markets.

BTC Longs Hold Through the Selloff

BTC perp longs on Hyperliquid are paying +10.4% APR at $63,932. At $2.28B in open interest (OI), that is roughly $651,000 per day transferred from long holders to short holders, every 24 hours, whether price moves or not. Notably, BTC funding rose from +9.7% APR in the 08:17 UTC snapshot to +10.4% APR now — longs added to their carry exposure as the Dow sold off, not out of it. See also how Fed rate decisions have historically affected crypto perp positioning.

Positive funding records that more notional is on the long side than the short side. The carry cost is the on-chain arithmetic of that imbalance — rising carry during an equity selloff means the long-side conviction on Hyperliquid increased as equities declined.

ETH's Quiet Intraday Reversal

ETH funding has drifted to −2.29% APR from +10.95% APR in the 08:17 UTC snapshot earlier today. The direction of payment has reversed: where ETH longs were paying ~$575K/day this morning, ETH shorts are now paying ~$117K/day to stay short. ETH slipped 1.2% in the session; BTC is effectively flat (+0.22%).

The magnitude of the current negative ETH funding is small (−0.0021% per 8h). It records a mild lean toward the short side, not a conviction positioning. Compare that to the −11.7% APR recorded in the 04:17 UTC snapshot today, where short-side pressure was much heavier.

What to Watch

  • Fed rate decision outcome: The Dow's 800-point drop reflects uncertainty priced into equities pre-announcement. BTC funding staying positive through the session suggests perp positioning is not pricing in the same level of risk-off. Watch whether BTC funding compresses toward zero post-decision.
  • ETH funding direction: At −0.0021%/8h, ETH shorts are paying a small carry. If that deepens toward −0.01%/8h, it indicates shorts are building a larger position. If it flips back positive, the intraday reversal unwound.
  • TradFi tokenization velocity: Visa, BNY, and Morgan Stanley all made public blockchain commitments in the same session. The pattern to watch is whether announcements like these start to co-move with crypto perp open interest — the on-chain equivalent of institutional demand showing up on Hyperliquid.
